Peer Review

In the interest of transparency, here are the results of every review invitation I have personally received as of November 2, 2026. Some takeaways:

  • I try to review as many papers as I can. I often decline them because I do not feel qualified.
  • I try to complete my first review within a week. Sometimes I take longer, but on average, I meet this goal.
  • Most of the papers I review are accepted. Some need very little revision. For others, I make a concerted effort to identify their merit and find an avenue towards publication. Papers that were rejected often had serious flaws.
  • I think that if everyone made similar efforts, the world would be a little better.
