Below are publicly available protocols for sample collection, nucleic acid extraction, amplicon library preparation, and sequence processing. Please contact me for additional methods not posted online.

## Sample collection

- [NOAA-CalCOFI Ocean Genomics (NCOG) Sample Collection](https://dx.doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.bmubk6sn)

## Nucleic acid extraction

Automated extractions from sterivex filters with an Eppendorf epMotion.

- [Sterivex DNA extraction](https://dx.doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.bc2hiyb6)
- [Sterivex RNA extraction](https://dx.doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.bd9ti96n)

## Amplicon library preparation

- [Amplicon Library Preparation](https://dx.doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.bmuck6sw) — 16S and 18S rRNA genes
- [Automated 96-well PCR Purification](https://www.protocols.io/view/automated-96-well-pcr-purification-bgzdjx26.html)

## Sequence processing

QIIME2 workflows for generating amplicon sequence variants (ASVs) with DADA2.

- [16S V4-V5 rRNA genes](https://github.com/allenlab/QIIME2_16S_ASV_protocol) — 515F-Y/926R primers; annotation with SILVA
- [18S V4 rRNA genes](https://github.com/allenlab/QIIME2_18Sv4_ASV_protocol) — V4F/V4RB primers; annotation with PR2
- [18S V9 rRNA genes](https://github.com/allenlab/QIIME2_18Sv9_ASV_protocol) — 1389F/1510R primers; annotation with PR2
